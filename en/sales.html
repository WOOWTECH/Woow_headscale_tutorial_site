Why your home needs a VPN tailnet of its own

Your home fills up with devices: a NAS, cameras, a printer, Home Assistant, phones, laptops. To reach them safely from outside, the traditional answer is to open a pile of ports and remember a pile of IP addresses — and the risk of doing that is high.

Tailscale and Headscale solve it with an encrypted peer-to-peer tailnet: devices connect to each other directly, with no public port to open and no IP address to remember. What Headscale adds is that control over "who can reach whom" comes back to you as well.

The one-line pitch: "An ordinary VPN rents someone else's gateway; Headscale builds your own. The connection is just as smooth — the difference is who holds the keys."

What Headscale is

Headscale is an open source, self-hosted implementation of the Tailscale coordination server. It is the brain that manages the whole tailnet (who is on the network, who can reach whom, keys and DNS), but the actual traffic still runs over encrypted peer-to-peer tunnels between the devices themselves (WireGuard).

The control server is in your home, and the network is yours to decide

  • The node list, the ACL and the keys all live on your own server, never on the official coordination server.
  • You issue the preauth keys and can revoke them at any time, so you decide entirely who joins the tailnet.
  • No monthly fee just because you have more devices; capacity comes down to your hardware and what you need.
Honest caveat: the benefit of self-hosting is control; the price is maintenance. WoowTech takes care of deployment, updates, backups and security, so you get the control without carrying the maintenance burden.

FAQ

Can a third party see my connections?
No. The control server is in your home, and data traffic runs as a direct, encrypted peer-to-peer connection between devices; traffic falls back to a DERP relay only when a direct connection cannot be made.
How is this different from an ordinary VPN?
An ordinary VPN is usually a central gateway; Headscale is a peer-to-peer mesh in which devices connect to each other directly, which suits a private tailnet with many devices much better.
Do I need to know how to set up a server?
No. On your side you only install the Tailscale client and use the app; WoowTech takes care of deployment and maintenance.
Is it secure?
Encrypted peer-to-peer connections + minimal exposure through the ACL + self-hosted control. Guard the preauth keys and the ACL, and the security level is yours to set.
Is there a limit on the number of devices?
It depends on your option. Self-hosted, it comes down to your hardware, and you can scale it as you need.
